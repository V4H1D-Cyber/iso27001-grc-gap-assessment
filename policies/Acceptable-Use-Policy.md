# Acceptable Use Policy

**Document owner:** Chief Technology Officer
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and third parties with access to Meridian FinTech Ltd (fictional) systems, devices, or data.
**Related controls:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 5.10 (Acceptable use of information and other associated assets)

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable and unacceptable use of company-owned or company-provisioned devices, accounts, networks, and data, to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company and customer information.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all company-issued laptops and mobile devices, all company email and SaaS accounts, all access to production and staging environments, and any personal device used to access company systems (bring-your-own-device).

## 3. Acceptable Use

- Company systems and accounts may be used for legitimate business purposes, and incidental, reasonable personal use (e.g. checking personal email) is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties or introduce security risk.
- All company devices must have disk encryption and up-to-date endpoint antivirus enabled at all times.
- Multi-factor authentication must be enabled on every account that supports it, without exception.
- Customer and company data must only be stored in approved systems (the production database, approved cloud storage, or the company's SSO-connected SaaS tools) — never on personal cloud storage, personal email, or unencrypted removable media.

## 4. Prohibited Use

- Sharing login credentials with any other person, including coworkers.
- Disabling or circumventing security controls (antivirus, disk encryption, MFA, VPN) on a company device.
- Installing unapproved software that requests elevated/administrator privileges without IT approval.
- Accessing, copying, or transmitting customer data for any purpose outside an employee's assigned job duties.
- Using company systems to engage in illegal activity, harassment, or any activity that violates company conduct policy.
- Connecting to production systems from unmanaged or unpatched personal devices.

## 5. Monitoring

The company reserves the right to monitor use of company-owned systems and accounts for security and compliance purposes, in accordance with applicable law. Employees should have no expectation of privacy regarding data stored on company-issued devices or accounts.

## 6. Reporting

Any suspected security incident, lost/stolen device, or observed violation of this policy must be reported immediately to the security point of contact (see Incident Response Policy, Section 4).

## 7. Enforcement

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination, consistent with the company's disciplinary process (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 6.4).

## 8. Review

This policy will be reviewed annually or following any significant security incident.
